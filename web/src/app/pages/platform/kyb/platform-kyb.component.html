Trace → structure → review → resolve

The same anatomy as the platform overview, applied specifically to vendor onboarding:

  1. Trace. Vendor-submitted data is pulled against the applicable rails and logged step by step.
  2. Structure. The trace resolves into a single risk-flag report — verified, review, or escalate — nothing inferred beyond what was checked.
  3. Review. An underwriter reads the report and the underlying trace, not a black-box score.
  4. Resolve. The underwriter closes the case. The Verified Line mark only resolves into a checkmark at this step — never before.
