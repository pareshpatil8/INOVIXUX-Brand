Non-commercial hold (INO-14)

Internal mandate (INO-14): no commercial or validation sign-off until the MVP is verified. This site describes a system under active verification, not a shipped product with validated outcomes. Nothing here is a pricing page, a customer claim, or a "live now" statement — see Legal → Disclosures for the same hold stated as a standalone page.

Accessibility — WCAG 2.2 AA

Both theme modes are independently audited against the WCAG relative-luminance formula, not eyeballed.

Contrast

Every text/on-fill pairing in both dark and light mode clears 4.5:1 (AA); most clear 7:1 (AAA). The one deliberate exception — the accent violet at 4.38:1 — is restricted by convention to non-text/large-text/decorative use.
Touch targets

Dense mode holds a 32px minimum row height (WCAG 2.2 §2.5.8's 24px floor, padded above it deliberately); fluid mode uses 44px, matching the common iOS/Android touch-target convention.
Motion

All hover/focus elevation and count-up animation respects prefers-reduced-motion — nothing here relies on motion to convey state.

Security & governance posture

Human-in-the-loop by design

The design system does not decide, approve, or auto-flag anything in the KYB risk-review flow — RAG status values are supplied by product logic and only rendered per an audited token contract. A human reviewer closes every case; the system's role stops at surfacing evidence.
