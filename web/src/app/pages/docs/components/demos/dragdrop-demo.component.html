Default

Drag with the mouse, or Tab to an item and press Space to pick it up, arrow keys or Tab to reach a zone, Enter to drop, Escape to cancel. Each move briefly shows the dropped-flash state. "Compliance sign-off" is disabled and cannot be dragged.

Capped target zone

maxItems rejects drops once the target zone is full (over-target feedback shows "full" instead of "available" to both mouse and keyboard users).

States

Disabled — neither zone accepts drag or drop interaction.
