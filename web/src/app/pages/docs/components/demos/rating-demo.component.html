Variants — star count and custom icons

"Cancelable" clears to 0 when you click the already-selected star — the §5 row's clear/cancel toggle (see SPEC.md §2). Values are always whole numbers (the row's "integer values" variant) — there is no half-star increment.

States

"Hover-preview" (the §5 row's state) is interaction-driven — hover any editable rating to see the preview fill up to the pointer without committing value.

Sizing — sm / md / lg

Density

dense

fluid

Stars are not a text-entry control and have no dense-register height row — they render identically in both densities (see SPEC.md §5).

Labels

The third rating has no visible label — its accessible name comes from the ariaLabel input (the §5 row's "own label" cell). "Value announced" falls out of native radiogroup semantics: focusing the checked star announces its label and position.

RTL spot-check

Arrow-key selection is deliberately unmirrored in RTL, matching the ino-knob/ ino-slider precedent (see the docs page's Accessibility contract).
