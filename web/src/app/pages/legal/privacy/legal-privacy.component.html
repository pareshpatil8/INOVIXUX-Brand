Legal / Privacy

Privacy

This site does not collect personal data beyond what's needed to run it. There is no live product surface here to describe a data-processing policy for yet.

What this site collects

This marketing/docs site has no account system, no analytics wired up, and no form submissions stored anywhere yet — the contact page routes to a mailto link, not a database. Nothing you do here is logged beyond standard web-server access logs.

What the product will collect

The KYB underwriting module handles vendor data (GSTIN, UPI, ABDM, MCA21 records) once it is live — that data-handling policy will be published here before, not after, the module leaves verification.
