## What "apart from the earlier" means concretely

Every mark shown across all prior rounds was built from one of four grammars:

1. line-and-node monograms — v1–v4 concepts and the shipped IX mark;
2. flat color-blocked solids — round 2's Ledger Fold and Convergence;
3. a single open stroke — round 2's Signal Vitals;
4. floating negative-space brackets — round 2's Axis Mark.

Round 3 deliberately uses four construction techniques that appear in **none** of the above:

| # | Name | Technique (new in this round) | Primary anchor | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Facet Core** | dimensional facet shading (isometric prism) — first non-flat mark of any round | core-banking solidity; three domains as three faces of one object | hex-prism marks exist in crypto branding; needs its lockup + exact facet ratios to de-genericize |
| 2 | **Resolve Matrix** | repetition/density field — dots growing along the diagonal until they resolve into a solid | the literal `-ix` matrix; complexity → clarity (the UX pitch itself) | needs a drawn (not scaled) 3×3 reduction for 16px — a two-master mark |
| 3 | **Meridian Shift** | slice-and-shift of a single solid — a disc cut on the diagonal, core band slid along the cut | complex systems held stable while the core piece is moved with surgical precision | protruding band tips make the silhouette non-circular; favicon/app-icon need a tighter master |
| 4 | **Triad Rotor** | rotational (120°) symmetry — three identical capsules around a negative-space core | three domains, one platform center; motion without an arrow | pinwheel genre is crowded; test against lookalikes before a finalist call |

The page shows each mark on dark, on light, and at true 16×16 favicon scale (round 2 feedback
surface — small-size legibility — is now visible up front instead of a written caveat).

## What's being asked

Per concept, same protocol as round 2:

1. **Kill** — doesn't work, don't spend more time on it.
2. **Keep exploring** — right direction, wrong execution (say what to change).
3. **Finalist** — push to full production (icon-on-dark/light, mono variants, favicon, lockup,
   letterhead, pitch deck, social card, `--ino-logo-mark` swap point at `/assets/brand/logo/`).

Verdicts can span rounds: e.g. "kill all of round 2, finalist round 3 #1" or "head-to-head
round 2 #4 vs round 3 #3" are both valid outcomes. If more than one concept is marked finalist,
next step is a head-to-head refinement round on just those.
