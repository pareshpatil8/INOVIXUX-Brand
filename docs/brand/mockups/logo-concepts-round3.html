Round 3 — four further logo directions

Requested by the board on 2026-09-23: further concepts apart from everything shown before. Every earlier mark was built from one of four grammars — line-and-node monograms (v1–v4 and the shipped IX mark), color-blocked flat cards, a single open stroke, or floating brackets. Round 3 uses four construction techniques none of those touched: dimensional facets, a particle field resolving into a solid, a solid sliced and shifted, and rotational symmetry. Third swatch on each card is the mark at true 16×16 favicon scale. Nothing here is implemented anywhere; pick, reject, or ask for a variant per concept below.

CONCEPT 1 / 4 · ROUND 3

Facet Core

dimensional faceted solid — three faces, one form

A hexagonal prism read in isometric: three flat facets in three depths of the brand violet, resolving into one solid object. Three faces for the three domains — payments, healthcare, complex systems — machined into a single bank-grade block. It is the first dimensional mark of any round: everything prior was flat. Reads as an object you could hold, not an icon from a template set.

core banking soliditythree domains, one forminnovation
Why it's apart from earlier rounds: facet-shaded dimensional construction — no prior concept had depth. No strokes, nodes, letterforms, or negative-space tricks. Risk: hexagon-cube marks exist in crypto branding; the three-tone violet ramp and precise facet ratios are what keep it ours — needs a custom wordmark lockup to fully de-genericize.
CONCEPT 2 / 4 · ROUND 3

Resolve Matrix

particle field resolving into a solid — complexity → clarity

A 5×5 dot matrix — the literal -ix — that grows and saturates along the diagonal until the field resolves into a solid mass. It is the company's whole pitch drawn in one gesture: scattered complexity on one side, resolved clarity on the other. The matrix also carries the data/ledger read for payments and the signal read for healthcare without depicting either literally.

-ix / matrixcomplexity → clarityUX as the product
Why it's apart from earlier rounds: built from a field of repeated units with a gradient of scale — no prior round used repetition or density as the construction. Risk: at 16px the 5×5 field needs the reduced 3×3 cut shown above (drawn, not scaled) — a two-master mark, which is more production work than a single-master one.
CONCEPT 3 / 4 · ROUND 3

Meridian Shift

solid disc, sliced — the core band drawn out with precision

One solid disc — the monolith every bank and hospital actually runs on — cut clean along the diagonal, its core band slid out along the cut. The violet band is the piece INOVIXUX touches: extracted, visible, moved with surgical precision while the system around it holds shape. Dynamic without an arrow, technical without a circuit.

complex systemssurgical precisionpayments in motion
Why it's apart from earlier rounds: slice-and-shift of a single solid — prior rounds composed multiple shapes or strokes; nothing was ever cut apart. Risk: the protruding band tips make the silhouette non-circular — favicon and app-icon crops need a dedicated tighter master.
CONCEPT 4 / 4 · ROUND 3

Triad Rotor

rotational symmetry — three domains around one still center

Three identical capsules at 120°, one per domain, orbiting a shared negative-space core. The center — the still point the whole mark organizes around — is the platform itself. Implied motion without an arrow; balance without symmetry along any single axis. The three violet depths keep each domain distinct while the geometry insists they are one system.

three domainsone platform coremotion / innovation
Why it's apart from earlier rounds: rotational (120°) construction — every earlier mark was built on a vertical, horizontal, or diagonal axis. Risk: pinwheel geometry is a crowded genre (fans, spinners); the capsule proportions and three-depth ramp carry the distinctiveness, and it should be tested against lookalikes before any finalist call.
