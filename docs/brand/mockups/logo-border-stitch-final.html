Border Stitch — master refinement & applications

The board approved R4-4 Border Stitch on 2026-09-24 — two territories, stitched into one settlement system. This round finalizes it: three master-geometry candidates (same concept, different tuning), then the application suite — wordmark lockups, app icon, favicon scale, one-color reductions — shown on the recommended master. One verdict per master candidate; when one is approved it becomes the production mark behind the single --ino-logo-mark swap point.

approved concept: border stitch cross-border settlement two systems, one platform bar: Apple / SpaceX / 21st.dev
MASTER A / 3 · RECOMMENDED

A — Approved geometry, trued

the round-4 mark, optically centered

Exactly what the board approved, with one correction: the stitch band sat 1 unit low in Round 4; here it is optically centered against the blocks. Nothing else moves. Lightest-touch path to production.

Trade-off: stitches touch the block edges but do not enter them; at very small sizes the thread can read as floating between the territories rather than binding them.
MASTER B / 3

B — Anchored stitches

the thread passes through the territories

Same blocks, but each stitch runs 3 units into both territories — sewn through, not laid across. The mark reads more like one bound object; the stitches are unmistakably structural.

Trade-off: busier interior on the blocks; the light-lavender thread over the violet block loses a little contrast on the overlap.
MASTER C / 3

C — Narrow border, heavy thread

denser mass, built for 16px

Wider territories, tighter border gap, heavier stitches. The most solid, monolithic read of the three — closest to the Apple-register single-object feel, and the most legible at favicon scale.

Trade-off: the narrower gap de-emphasizes the "border" itself; the two-territories story is quieter, the one-object story louder.
