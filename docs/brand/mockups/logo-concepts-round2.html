INOVIXUX · INO-82 · logo concepts — round 2 (dedicated workstream, split from INO-31)

Four structurally different logo concepts

Not palette or geometry variations of one idea — four different construction techniques (solid fill, open stroke pictogram, bracket/reticle, overlapping tiles), each grounded in a different one of the brand's anchors. The shipped IX monogram (commit f1b3358) stays live and untouched — this is a parallel exploration round, not a replacement in progress. Nothing here is implemented anywhere; pick, reject, or ask for a variant per concept below.

CONCEPT 1 / 4

Ledger Fold

two-tone solid card, dog-eared corner

A single reconciled page — split violet/indigo down the diagonal (two ledgers, one record) with a folded corner, the universal mark of a document. Reads instantly at 16px as a "record" glyph without being a generic folder or file icon: the diagonal split and the peeled corner are load-bearing, not decoration.

paymentscore bankingreconciliation
Why it's different: solid color-blocked shape, zero strokes/nodes/letterforms — opposite construction technique from the shipped IX mark and from every prior rejected round (all of which were line-and-node monograms).
CONCEPT 2 / 4

Signal Vitals

pulse waveform resolving into a circuit trace

One unbroken stroke: it opens as an ECG heartbeat (healthcare), then turns a hard right angle into a stepped PCB-style trace (systems infrastructure), and lands on a solid "verified" node. It's a live signal, not an icon of a body part or a chip — literally two of the four anchors fused into one continuous line.

healthcarecomplex systemslive/real-time
Why it's different: asymmetric, irregular open path (no radial or bilateral symmetry) — none of the six prior rounds used an organic waveform; this is the only concept where the two halves of the mark are not mirror images of each other.
CONCEPT 3 / 4

Axis Mark

opposing corner brackets, origin node

Two viewfinder/crop-mark corners at opposite ends of a frame that's never fully drawn, with a single resolved point sitting exactly where their axes would cross. It's the most literal read of "-ix" as a coordinate axis (x/y), and of UX as "where the user's aim meets the system's precision" — telemetry-reticle register, closest of the four to the SpaceX/mission-patch benchmark named for this round.

-ix / axisUX / precisioninnovation
Why it's different: the mark is mostly negative space — two small corner strokes and a dot, no continuous outline and no filled silhouette. Most minimal of the four; riskiest at favicon scale (flagged below).
CONCEPT 4 / 4

Convergence

two overlapping tiles, no crossing lines

Two rounded tiles — one indigo, one violet — offset on the diagonal so they overlap into a single lit seam. Reads as separate systems (payments, banking, healthcare, whatever the client stack is) merging into one product surface, and doubles as the cleanest possible app-icon silhouette of the four at 16–32px.

complex systemsintegrationproduct
Why it's different: no crossing diagonals anywhere in the mark — deliberately avoids the X/checkmark-crossing grammar used by the shipped mark and by every v1–v4 rejected concept, since that grammar is the one repeat pattern across six prior rounds.
