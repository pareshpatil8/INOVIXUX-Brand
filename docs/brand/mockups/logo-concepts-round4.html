Round 4 — four marks built from payments primitives

Board redirect on 2026-09-24: "can we create the logo for a company which is in payment systems, cross border payments, payment orchestrations, reconciliation etc." Every earlier round drew abstract geometry and mapped meaning onto it afterwards. Round 4 inverts that: each mark is a named payments mechanic — interchange (two flows exchanging), reconciliation (two ledgers closing into one), orchestration (a payment routed across rails), and cross-border settlement (two territories stitched into one system). No coins, globes, arrows, or checkmarks — the domain is expressed structurally, at the Apple / SpaceX register, not with fintech clip-art. Third swatch on each card is the mark at true 16×16 favicon scale. Verdict per concept below.

CONCEPT 1 / 4 · ROUND 4

Interchange

two payment flows exchanging lanes — the X is the transaction

Two value flows enter from opposite sides and swap positions in one smooth motion, one passing over the other. The crossing point is the transaction — "interchange" is the literal industry word for it. The weave forms the X of the -ix suffix without typing a letter: money out, money in, cross-border by construction. One gesture, two colors, no decoration.

interchange / exchangecross-borderthe -ix X
Why it's apart from earlier rounds: woven over-under flow ribbons — no prior round wove anything; earlier stroke work was a single open line, never two flows exchanging. Risk: crossing-path marks exist in logistics branding; the over-under weave, exact curvature, and two-tone violet are the ownable part. Reads perfectly at 16px.
CONCEPT 2 / 4 · ROUND 4

Ledger Seam

two ledgers closing into one solid — reconciliation as a lock

Two ledger halves — ours and theirs, debit and credit — meet on a stepped seam and lock into one flawless block. Nothing is missing, nothing overlaps: the seam only closes when every entry matches. That is reconciliation, drawn. The rectilinear step (not a puzzle knob) keeps it bank-grade; the closed block says the books balanced.

reconciliationtwo parties, one truthcore banking solidity
Why it's apart from earlier rounds: complementary counterform halves locking into a solid — earlier solids were sliced apart (Meridian Shift) or color-blocked; nothing ever joined. Risk: interlock marks can drift toward "puzzle piece"; the hard rectilinear seam and single-object silhouette avoid that genre. Strong at 16px — it is one solid shape.
CONCEPT 3 / 4 · ROUND 4

Orchestra Rails

one payment, three rails, the smart route lit

Three payment rails run in parallel — card, RTGS, wallet, whichever three you like. One live payment enters on the top rail and is routed, mid-flight and without a seam, to the rail that clears it best. That single lit path across dim rails is payment orchestration: the platform chooses the route, the money never notices. The mark is calm, horizontal, infrastructural.

payment orchestrationsmart routingrails / infrastructure
Why it's apart from earlier rounds: a lit route over dim infrastructure — two visual weights in one mark; every earlier concept was single-weight. No nodes, no arrowheads. Risk: lane-change curves appear in transit and routing iconography; the three-rail cage and weight contrast are the ownable part. Verify legibility of the dim rails on very low-quality displays before finalist.
CONCEPT 4 / 4 · ROUND 4

Border Stitch

two territories, stitched into one settlement system

Two solid blocks — two countries, two currencies, two banking systems — and three clean stitches binding them across the border. Cross-border payments as structure: not an arrow flying over a gap, but infrastructure that makes two territories behave as one. The stitches lean forward, giving the mark quiet motion without an arrowhead anywhere.

cross-border paymentssettlement corridorstwo systems, one platform
Why it's apart from earlier rounds: a thread binding two solids — earlier rounds never combined solid geometry with line work in one mark. Risk: stitching can read as textile/apparel out of context; the rigid twin blocks and diagonal rhythm keep it infrastructural. At 16px the three stitches stay countable.
