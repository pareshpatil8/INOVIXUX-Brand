Colors — Primary

The single swap point for a mark change (--ino-color-accent / --ino-gradient-accent / --ino-glow-accent) plus its safe derivatives. accent is decorative/non-text only — accent-text-safe is the AA-verified role for accent text and filled buttons. Source: tokens.css §2.
