Reduced motion

With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, the two live component demos below (spinner spin, skeleton shimmer) stop entirely rather than continuing at a different speed — durations are never zeroed globally, since that would also kill non-motion transitions components rely on for state (focus rings, etc).
