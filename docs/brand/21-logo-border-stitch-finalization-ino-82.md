# Border Stitch Finalization — Round 5 (INO-82)

**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Status:** proposed — awaiting per-master board verdict
**Rendered page:** `docs/brand/mockups/logo-border-stitch-final.html` (hosted at `/brand/mockups/logo-border-stitch-final.html` on the Pages site)

## Where this comes from

On 2026-09-24 the board answered the Round 4 verdict card: **R4-4 Border Stitch
approved**; R4-1 Interchange, R4-2 Ledger Seam and R4-3 Orchestra Rails rejected
("not inline with brand"). Border Stitch is the first approved concept across
the full logo workstream (8+ rejected rounds on INO-31/INO-82).

## What Round 5 asks

One verdict per **master candidate** — three tunings of the same approved
concept, not new concepts:

| # | Name | What changes vs the approved R4-4 |
|---|------|-----------------------------------|
| A | **Approved geometry, trued** (recommended) | stitch band optically centered (was 1 unit low); nothing else moves |
| B | **Anchored stitches** | stitches run 3 units into both blocks — sewn through, not laid across |
| C | **Narrow border, heavy thread** | wider blocks, tighter gap, heavier stitches; densest at 16px |

The page also shows the application suite (horizontal + stacked lockups, app
icon, 32/16 favicon, one-color reductions) built on Master A; approving a
master approves the suite regenerated from it.

## What happens after approval

The winning master becomes the production mark behind the single
`--ino-logo-mark` swap point: SVG/PNG exports, favicon, letterhead, pitch deck,
social card. The shipped IX monogram stays live and untouched until that
implementation issue lands. Implementation will be cut as a separate child
issue once the master is ratified.
