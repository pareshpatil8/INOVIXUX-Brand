IftaLabel — in-field top-aligned label wrapper

Permanently docks a small label to the field's top-inside edge (INO-142). Unlike <ino-float-label>, there is no rest/floated toggle to derive — the label always renders in the docked position, matching the single style PrimeNG's own IftaLabel route ships (see SPEC.md §1/§3).
