Nested submenus

Items with children render a chevron (›) and open a submenu on hover (desktop) or first tap (touch). Submenu anchors to the parent's inline-end edge (right in LTR, left in RTL).

States

Roving tabindex (only one item is tabindex="0" at a time). Arrow keys move focus. Disabled items have aria-disabled="true" and are skipped by keyboard navigation. Separators are role="separator" and never focusable.
