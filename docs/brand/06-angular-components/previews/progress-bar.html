ProgressBar — process status indicator

Determinate (0–100, animated fill) and indeterminate (looping sweep) process status. Decorative and non-interactive — role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow in determinate mode, aria-busy in indeterminate mode. See web/src/app/components/progress-bar/SPEC.md for the full accessibility contract.
