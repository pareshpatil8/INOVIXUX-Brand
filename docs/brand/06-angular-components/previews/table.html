Motion

Header hover, sort-icon rotation, reorder-control opacity, row hover, and the expand/group chevron all transition on --ino-motion-duration-fast + --ino-motion-easing-standard (aliased to a local --_t custom property in the component to stay inside the anyComponentStyle budget — see SPEC.md §9). Every transition collapses under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — toggle your OS setting to see both branches.
