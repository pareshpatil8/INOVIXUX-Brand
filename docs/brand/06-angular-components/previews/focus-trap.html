FocusTrap

A behaviour-only primitive: it paints nothing. It confines Tab / Shift+Tab to a container and releases focus when suspended — WCAG 2.2 SC 2.1.2, which is about release as much as containment. Consumed by Modal, Drawer, Popover and ConfirmDialog.

Containment

Focus the first button below, then hold Tab. Focus cycles inside the panel and never reaches the “outside” control — until you suspend the trap.

Verify entity

Tab past the last control and focus wraps to the first.

Link
Focus: — · trap active

The two tab stops that do the wrapping are aria-hidden, zero-opacity sentinel spans seated as the container’s first and last child — a screen-reader virtual cursor never meets them. The trap adds no role or ARIA of its own; role="dialog" belongs to the consuming surface, as does Esc.
