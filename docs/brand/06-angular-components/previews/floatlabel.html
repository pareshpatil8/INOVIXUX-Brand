FloatLabel — label-placement wrapper

Makes label placement a wrapper concern instead of a per-control prop (INO-141). Three variants ported from the PrimeNG benchmark: over, in, on. "Floated" is derived from focus/value, the same as the shipped component (see SPEC.md §1 for why this is JS-driven rather than a CSS-only :focus/:not(:placeholder-shown) trick).

Variants × theme

dark — :root (default)

light — [data-theme="light"]

high-contrast — [data-theme="high-contrast"]

RTL check
