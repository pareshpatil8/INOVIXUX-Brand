02 · Using the mark

Variants, clear space, minimum sizes

One-color (mono) versions

In one-color reproduction the stitches are knocked out — the substrate shows through. Never print the stitches as a second tint in a one-color job.

Clear space

Keep a margin of at least the width of one territory (13/40 of the mark's width) free of other elements on all sides of the mark or lockup.

Minimum sizes & the heavy-thread cut

ContextMinimumRule
Mark, digital16 pxAt ≤ 20 px use the heavy-thread cut (stroke 3.4) — favicon / app-glyph masters
Mark, print5 mmAt ≤ 7 mm use the heavy-thread cut
Lockup, print22 mm wideBelow this, use the mark alone

Don'ts

  • Don't rotate, skew, outline, or add effects (shadows, gradients) to the mark.
  • Don't recolor the territories or thread outside the specified palette.
  • Don't place the full-color mark on mid-tone or busy backgrounds — switch to a mono version.
  • Don't rebuild the geometry from this document — do place the supplied SVG/PDF masters.

04 · Typography

Type system

Display & body — Geist

Evidence a human acts on.

Aa Bb Cc Dd Ee Ff Gg Hh Ii Jj Kk Ll Mm 0123456789

Weights in use: 400 (body), 500, 600 (headings), 700 (display). Fallback stack: system-ui, Arial, Helvetica.

Technical & labels — Geist Mono

VERIFICATION · TRACE ID 0x4F46E5 · 0123456789

Used for eyebrows, data, timestamps, and identifiers — always with tracking (+8 to +14%) when uppercase.

Devanagari — Noto Sans Devanagari

सत्यापन जो मानव निर्णय का समर्थन करता है — इनोविक्सक्स

Paired with Geist in the fallback chain for Hindi/Marathi surfaces (INO-119). Match Latin x-height optically; never fake-bold.

Rules

  • Headlines are sentence case; never all-caps except Geist Mono eyebrows/labels.
  • Tight letter-spacing (−1 to −2%) on display sizes only; body text is never negative-tracked.
  • Do not substitute look-alike fonts (Inter, Roboto) in partner materials — request the font files instead.

05 · Voice

How INOVIXUX speaks

We describe a system that surfaces evidence for a human to act on. We never describe a system that decides. Every sentence must survive the question: does this imply the software made the call, or that a person did? If it implies the software decided, it is factually wrong — human review is a product guarantee, not a caveat.

Anchor vocabulary

UseForNever substitute
VerificationThe core product action — checking, cross-referencing, tracing"validation" (approval is the human's job)
Augmented ControlThe reviewer ↔ system relationship"automation", "AI-powered decisioning"
GovernanceCompliance posture, audit-readiness, DPI alignment"control" (implies automation)
ResilienceSystem/infra reliability, uptime, audit trails"robust", "bulletproof"
Traced / resolvedA data point moving through the system"flagged and closed" (system doesn't close)

In practice

  • Do: "INOVIXUX traces the discrepancy and surfaces it for your reviewer."
  • Don't: "INOVIXUX automatically approves compliant merchants."
  • Plain, specific, calm. No exclamation marks in product or partner copy.
