# Logo Concepts — Round 4 (INO-82): payments-domain marks

**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Status:** proposed — awaiting per-concept board verdicts
**Rendered page:** `docs/brand/mockups/logo-concepts-round4.html` (hosted at `/brand/mockups/logo-concepts-round4.html` on the Pages site)

## The brief change

On 2026-09-24 the board rejected all four Round 3 concepts. Three were rejected as
"not going with brand value"; the fourth verdict carried the actual redirect:

> "can we create the logo for a company which is in payment systems, cross border
> payments, payment orchestrations, reconciliation etc."

Rounds 1–3 drew abstract geometry first and mapped meaning onto it afterwards.
Round 4 inverts the method: each mark **is** a named payments mechanic, expressed
structurally — no coins, globes, arrows, or checkmarks.

## The four concepts

| # | Name | Payments primitive | Construction grammar (new to this round) |
|---|------|--------------------|------------------------------------------|
| R4-1 | **Interchange** | interchange / cross-border exchange | two flow ribbons swapping lanes, woven over-under; the crossing forms the -ix X |
| R4-2 | **Ledger Seam** | reconciliation | two complementary ledger halves locking on a stepped seam into one solid block |
| R4-3 | **Orchestra Rails** | payment orchestration / smart routing | one lit route crossing three dim parallel rails — two visual weights in one mark |
| R4-4 | **Border Stitch** | cross-border settlement | a thread binding two solid blocks (territories) with three forward-leaning stitches |

Each concept is shown on dark, on light, and at true 16×16 favicon scale, with an
honest risk note (lookalike genres, small-size caveats) on its card.

## Prior-round grammar exclusion list

Round 4 avoids every construction used before: line-and-node monograms (v1–v4 +
shipped IX mark), flat color-blocked solids, single open strokes, floating
brackets (rounds 1–2); dimensional facets, dot-matrix resolution, sliced-and-
shifted solids, rotational capsule symmetry (round 3).

## Verdict protocol

Per concept — **kill / keep exploring (say what to change) / finalist** — via the
interaction card on INO-82 or a plain comment. Cross-concept remixes are valid
verdicts. The shipped IX monogram remains live and untouched until a finalist is
ratified and implemented behind the single `--ino-logo-mark` swap point.

## Round ledger

- Round 2 (Ledger Fold / Signal Vitals / Axis Mark / Convergence): superseded 2026-09-24 by the payments-domain redirect; verdict card withdrawn, concepts closed without individual verdicts.
- Round 3 (Facet Core / Resolve Matrix / Meridian Shift / Triad Rotor): all four rejected 2026-09-24.
